Additional information about accommodations and getting around Seattle will be added later.

Air travel

Travelers may be able to fly into Seattle–Tacoma International Airport (SEA, also called Sea-Tac) or Seattle Paine Field International Airport (PAE) in Everett. Paine Field has a more limited selection of flights.

Please note: Transportation from Paine Field to the University of Washington may be more difficult. Check ground transportation options before booking a flight to PAE.

Parking

Parking on the University of Washington campus is limited. See UW Transportation Services for current visitor parking information, including locations, payment options, and rates.
